---
title: "Get quotes"
description: "Returns the private and standard routes for a token pair and amount."
openapi: "GET /quotes"
---

Pick the mode with `types`:

- `types=standard`: direct single-leg swap. Fastest, no privacy hop
- `types=private`: two legs through a privacy bridge (XMR by default), so deposit and payout are not linkable on-chain

Without `types`, DEX routes come back too.

`from` and `to` are token ids; look them up with [Get tokens](/api-reference/private-and-standard-swaps/get-tokens). Pass the chosen `quoteId` to [Create exchange](/api-reference/private-and-standard-swaps/create-exchange) right away.
